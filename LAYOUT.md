# Layout

What each part of the repo is, with the pinned SHA-256 of every vendored file. Back to the [README](README.md).

- `app/`: page sources
  - `shell.html`: the page shell, its CSS and the settings defaults (`window.TWEAK_DEFAULTS`)
  - `game-app.jsx`: the app (the stored game or board, loading games, the dock and menus, the dialogs); `settings-tabs.jsx` the Settings panel's tabs and the engine settings; `share-dialog.jsx` Share and export and the export settings; `tweaks.jsx` the Settings controls
  - `engine.jsx`: the animation engine (the replay is one element tree drawn as a function of time); `game-video-core.jsx` the replay's scenes, the board's scaled space (`BoardSpace`) and text sizing (`tx()`)
  - `layout-core.js`: the page's layout (`LayoutCore`: one or two columns, the boxes for the board and panels, the export scale)
  - `chess-core.js`: PGN, analysis and notes turned into the game model the replay draws
  - `analysis-core.js`, `sf-engine.js`, `stockfish.jsx`: Stockfish in the browser (a Web Worker over UCI) and the move ratings, ported from chess-animator's Python
  - `move-eval.js`: the evals of a move for the Analysis panel (`MoveEval`: the searches of the positions before and after it, the cloud's answers, Go deeper, the session's finished searches), with the engine and the cloud passed in; `useMoveEval` in `stockfish.jsx` subscribes to it
  - `sf-full.js`: the full engine the viewer can download (the Analysis panel's engine drop-down), checked and kept in the browser
  - `openings-core.js`: opening names and book continuations (the opening explorer)
  - `lichess-core.js`: Lichess's public data (URLs, parsers, a polite client); `explorer.jsx` the Lichess opening explorer (Settings › Online); `tablebase.jsx` the endgame tablebase; `import-core.js` what Import game's paste box holds (a Lichess link, a FEN, a PGN)
  - `board-core.js`, `free-board.jsx`: the free board (Menu › Explore); `play-core.js` its games (rules, clocks, takebacks, the end); `games-core.js` My games
  - `edit-core.js`, `edit-board.jsx`: Edit position (its rules and its board)
  - `draw-core.js`: the viewer's own circles and arrows on the board
  - `dead-core.js`, `dead.jsx`, `dead-worker.js`: dead positions, proven by chasolver in a Worker
  - `vision-core.js`, `vision.js`, `vision-worker.js`, `vision-grid.js`, `vision.jsx`: Position from image (the board reader's files, the reader and its Worker, the board auto-crop, the dialog)
  - `kept-files.js`, `kept-download.jsx`: files downloaded only on the viewer's click and kept (Full Stockfish, the board reader), and their download UI
  - `queen-core.js`, `queen.jsx`: Ask Queen and its Hugging Face sign-in
  - `pkce.js`: the OAuth2 + PKCE steps the Hugging Face and Lichess sign-ins share
  - `export.jsx`, `webp-core.js`: MP4, WebM and animated WebP export; `export-draw.js` paints each export frame layer by layer, handing html2canvas only what it can't draw
  - `sfx-core.js`, `sfx.jsx`: the move sounds
  - `piece-sets.js`: the piece sets (Settings › View › Pieces): their credits, which are kept out of exports, and loading the chosen one
  - `board-themes.js`: the boards and their Brightness, Contrast, Tint, Saturation and Hue sliders (Settings › View), and drawing a picture board through them
  - `drag-scroll.js`: a mouse drag scrolls whatever scrolls under it
  - `sample-analysis.js`: the sample game's analysis, trimmed
  - `sw.js`: the site's service worker (offline); `icons/`, `og-image.png`: web app icons and the link-preview image
- `src/`: files from the original Claude Design project (`pieces.js`, `sample-game.js`, and `game-data.js`, which writes the sample game's commentary, `window.SAMPLE_NOTES`, all built into the page); `pieces/cburnett/` are Colin M.L. Burnett's pieces (GPL-2.0-or-later, as in Lichess)
- `babel/babel.min.js`: @babel/standalone 7.29.0, used only to precompile the JSX (sha256 `2623a9e22809915ce789b4461154e277ddce520d5a4320c14d44332a5d0dcea0`)
- `vision/`: the board reader's files (Position from image), plain git, not LFS.
