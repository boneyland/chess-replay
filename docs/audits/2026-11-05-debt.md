# Audit 2026-11-05: debt
Commit: 7cce52e · Areas: 6 · Findings: 6/35/38 · Dropped on checking: 0 (1 moved from high to medium; 9 duplicates across areas merged)

## Summary
The cores are in good shape and well tested; the debt sits in three places. First, **rules and formats written more than once**: two FEN readers, two PGN writers, two `[%clk]` formatters, the mate-score encoding in five files, and the "move has settled" time in four. Several of these copies have already drifted (castling in FEN, the Outro cap, clock tenths). Second, **two very large view closures**: `GameApp` (~790 lines, about ten jobs) and `GameVideo`, plus UI pieces copied between the replay and the free board (player bar, move chips, keydown guards). Third, **lists kept in step by hand in the build**: cache names, CDN hosts, served files and pins, some with no test. The audit also turned up one real crash (`GameStatus` reading an `sf` that isn't in scope) and one visible formatting bug (`1:60`).
