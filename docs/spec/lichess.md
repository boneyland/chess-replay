# Lichess data

[Spec](../spec.md) › what is built. What the page asks Lichess for, each behind a Settings › Online toggle: the endgame tablebase, the cloud evaluation and the opening explorer. Lichess game links are in [Import](import.md).

## Endgame tablebase

Positions of 7 pieces or fewer get Lichess's Syzygy tablebase (`https://tablebase.lichess.ovh/standard?fen=<FEN>`, no auth): the result with best play, its distances and every move ranked by result.

- `LichessCore` (`app/lichess-core.js`): the URL, the parser (`category`, `dtz`, `dtm`, `checkmate`, `stalemate`, `insufficient_material`, `moves[]` with `uci`, `san`, `category`, `dtz`, `dtm`, `zeroing`, as verified against the service), Lichess's verdicts, the eval bar's value and the moves grouped by result. Its client caches 300 answers by FEN (without the move number), sends one request at a time, backs off 60 s after an HTTP 429 and gives up after 10 s; an error, a 429 or being offline answers null ("Not available"), never an error screen. The FEN is the one Copy FEN gives (`ChessCore.fenAfter`): real castling rights, en passant square and fifty-move count, which the service takes into account (KBN v K with DTZ 55 is a win with the count at 0, a cursed win at 60).
- `app/tablebase.jsx`: `useTablebase(pos, on)` asks 300 ms after the last position change (a cached position shows at once), only at 7 pieces or fewer.
- Where: the Analysis panel's **Tablebase** block, above Stockfish's analysis in every layout: under the move and its verdict and above the engine lines (on the viewer's own move too), and above the analysis's progress where there is no analysis yet, so it shows without scrolling the panel. Its results always take two rows' height (more scroll inside it, fewer leave room; the same while looking up), so the lines under it don't move from one position to the next. The verdict in Lichess's words (White wins, Draw, Cursed win for White · drawn by the 50-move rule, Blessed loss…, the uncertain "maybe" ones too) with DTZ and DTM, then the moves in one line per result (Win, Draw, Loss), best first, each a button that plays it (in the replay as the viewer's own move, on the free board as a drag would) with its distances in its tooltip. On the free board it isn't in the Analysis panel but in the Commentary box, in place of the opening explorer (as on Lichess; `TablebaseExplorer`), titled **Endgame tablebase** with DTZ and DTM on the right: the verdict as the heading (a long one's reason as a note), then the moves grouped by result (Win, Draw, Loss), each group's moves wrapping in rows of SAN buttons with nothing else written beside them (their distances in their tooltips), so the losing ones, which can throw a draw away, show too; "Looking up…" while it's asked, "Not available" when Lichess can't be reached. Above 7 pieces or with the setting off the explorer is back. The eval bar shows the result instead of the eval, tagged TB: a win as a mate in DTM/2 moves (`TB M28`, `TB 1-0` without DTM), a draw, cursed win or blessed loss as `TB 0.00`; an uncertain result leaves the engine's eval.
- When: the replay while paused (never in playback or the video export), and the free board; never during a game (vs human or Stockfish). The stored analysis, ratings and graph are left alone.
- Settings › **Online** › **Endgame tablebase** (`TWEAK_DEFAULTS.tablebase = true`, as it fires only at 7 pieces or fewer); off sends nothing. `sw.js` never keeps its answers (live data, not in `KEEP_HOSTS`).
- Not done from the plan: no arrow colour for winning moves (the Stockfish arrow already points at a best move, and a second one crowded the board).
- Tests: `tests/lichess-core.test.cjs` (real answers in `tests/fixtures/tb-*.json`: a win, a draw, a cursed win, a mate; the piece-count gate, the cache, errors not cached, the 429 back-off, one request at a time), live: KBN v K on the free board shows the verdict and `TB M28`, a tablebase move plays, 8 pieces and the setting off send nothing.

**In game analysis** (PR 2b, built 2026-10-25): "Analyse with Stockfish" with the setting on asks the tablebase for each position of 7 pieces or fewer before searching it, through the page's one client (`TB` in `app/tablebase.jsx`: its cache, one request at a time, the 429 back-off).
- A game analysis whose tablebase request fails (not a 429 back-off, which ends) asks no more and searches the rest; `runAnalysis`'s `onTablebaseLost` then has the Analysis panel (above its lines) and the Analyse the game dialog say "Lichess’s tablebase stopped answering: Stockfish searched the endgame positions after that" until the next run starts.
- `AnalysisCore.runAnalysis({ tablebase })` (the client, or null with the setting off): a certain answer becomes the position's result, `{ cp, depth: 0, pv, best, tb: { category, dtz, dtm } }`: its eval `LichessCore.evalCp` (the eval bar's value: a win as a mate in DTM/2, ±9990 without DTM, a draw, cursed win or blessed loss 0; a checkmate ±10000, a stalemate 0), its best line the tablebase's best move alone (one request a position: the DTZ mainline, `/standard/mainline`, was built and dropped, see the decisions log). An uncertain answer ("maybe", "unknown") is searched. A run's requests go at least `AnalysisCore.TB_GAP_MS` (1 s) apart, an answer the client already has not waiting: Lichess publishes no limit for the tablebase, only one request at a time and a minute's wait after a 429 (see the decisions log, 2026-11-01). During the client's 60 s back-off after a 429 the positions are searched, then it is asked again; the first request that fails otherwise (offline, an error, the 10 s timeout) ends the asking for that run, and Stockfish searches the rest. The setting turned off during a run ends the asking too (`runAnalysis({ tablebaseOn })`, checked again after the wait between requests), so nothing more is sent; the run goes on with Stockfish.
- Ratings: a move from one tablebase position to another is rated by `AnalysisCore.rateByOutcome`: keeping the win, the draw or the loss is `best` with no loss, however slow; win → draw (a cursed win counts as a draw), draw → loss or win → loss is a `blunder`, its eval loss as `rateMove`'s (capped at 1500), no mate advice. A move between a searched position and a tablebase one (a capture into the tablebase, an uncertain answer, the back-off) is rated by the usual rules; where the tablebase side is a win or loss (`AnalysisCore.tbDecided`: a mate with its DTM, or with no DTM at 6 and 7 pieces, whose ±9990 means won, not mate in 1), that position is searched too (kept as the result's `sf`) and the move is rated on the two searches, as without the tablebase: Stockfish reads many tablebase mates as a small edge (KBN v K about +1.9), so a move that keeps the win next to a searched position was otherwise "Lost forced checkmate sequence". The eval shown stays the tablebase's.
- JSON: a move gets `tb_before` / `tb_after` (`{ category, dtz, dtm }`, each for its side to move) for the positions the tablebase answered, their search depths 0; the engine block gets `tablebase: true` when any position was; `ChessCore.buildGame` turns these into `ply.tb`, `ply.tbBefore`, `ply.won` (a win with no DTM: the eval shows `1-0`/`0-1`, not a mate count) and `ply.search = { tablebase: true }`, which the Analysis panel's header shows as "Tablebase" in place of a depth; the engine summary ends "· Lichess tablebase"; a best move from the tablebase says "The tablebase agrees". Go deeper isn't offered on those positions.
- A stopped run's searches (`sfPartialKey`) are keyed with the setting when it is on, so it resumes only with the same setting; a key with it off is as before. The estimate counts the game's positions of 7 pieces or fewer at 1 s each (`SF_TB_SECONDS`, one request each). The run's pace counts only the engine's searches and their time: `onSearched(R, { searches, tbMs })` gives the searches made and the time spent on the tablebase (requests and the waits between them) since the last call, so an uncertain answer's search isn't timed with its request.
- Never in Play vs computer (its opponent and critique don't use `runAnalysis`) or for the viewer's own-move ratings.
- Tests: `tests/tablebase-analysis.test.cjs` (fake client: no search for tablebase positions, fields, the outcome table, a cursed win, an uncertain or failed answer searched and the asking stopped, a 429 back-off searched through and asked again, the requests' pacing, the setting off and 8 pieces send nothing, the setting turned off mid-run, a win without DTM beside a searched position rated on two searches, the tablebase's time reported apart, a resumed run, `buildGame`); `tests/check_browser_json.py` also loads its JSON (`tests/out/tb_analysis.json`) in Python, which has no tablebase.

## Lichess cloud evaluation

Built 2026-10-27 (PR 3 of Lichess data): the positions Stockfish searches for the viewer's own moves also ask Lichess's cloud (`https://lichess.org/api/cloud-eval?fen=<FEN>&multiPv=<N>`, no auth), which keeps deep evaluations of many positions.

- The API as verified 2026-10-27: `{ fen, knodes, depth, pvs: [{ moves, cp | mate }] }`, scores from White's side (as the page's), `pvs` as many as it has up to `multiPv` (fewer is not a miss), castling written as the king taking its rook (`e1h1`), the clocks ignored; a position it never analysed is HTTP 404 `{ error }`. CORS open.
- `LichessCore` (`app/lichess-core.js`): `parseCloud(json, fen)` → the engine-result shape (`cp`, `depth`, `pv`, `best`, `lines: [{ k, cp, depth, pv }]`, `nodes`), tagged `source: 'cloud'`; a mate as ±(10000 − 10·N); `cloudMoves` turns the king-takes-rook castling into the board's `e1g1` and stops a line at the first illegal move. The client's `cloudEval(fen, lines)` shares the tablebase's machinery under its own gate (one request at a time, its own 429 back-off, the 10 s timeout), caches by board, side, castling, en passant and line count, and caches a 404 as null, so a miss is never asked again; `cloudCached` shows a known answer at once.
- `MoveEval` (`app/move-eval.js`, which `useMoveEval` in `app/stockfish.jsx` subscribes to) with `cloud: { on, prefer }`: each of its positions (before and after the viewer's move, the line count as the search's) asks the cloud 300 ms after the last change (at once when cached) while the local search runs; whichever comes first shows, and a finished local search replaces the cloud's answer only when deeper. A cloud answer is final, so the move is rated as soon as both positions have one. **Prefer cloud** skips the local search of a position the cloud has at least as deep as the depth limit, or depth 35 with No limit (`AnalysisCore.cloudEnough`: past what a timed search reaches in the browser); for the asm.js engine 22, which is also its cap under a deeper limit (in a 60 s middlegame search it reached 22 on a fast laptop, 19 on the Samsung SM-T510). Go deeper isn't offered when both positions show a cloud answer at least as deep as its next search's depth, or that 35 (asm.js: 22) with No limit (a press would change nothing). A miss, an error or a back-off falls back silently.
- Shown as Stockfish's result: eval, lines, arrows, the eval bar; the Analysis title row says **cloud depth 60** in the eval's row (the tooltip "Lichess cloud, depth 60"), the lines' tooltips "Lichess cloud's line".
- Where: the free board and moves the viewer tries in the replay; on a loaded analysis's move only once Go deeper is pressed (the analysis's eval stands until then; the cloud's answer shows if deeper than it, its line as the Best line and its eval on the eval bar too, and the ratings, graph and accuracy stay the analysis's, as with a local Go deeper). Never for "Analyse with Stockfish", the Stockfish opponent or its critique, nor during a game.
- Settings › **Online** › **Lichess cloud evaluation** (`TWEAK_DEFAULTS.cloudEval = false`) and, under it, **Prefer cloud** (`cloudPrefer = false`); off sends nothing. `lichess.org` added to the CSP's `connect-src`; `sw.js` passes it through (not in `KEEP_HOSTS`).
- Tests: `tests/lichess-core.test.cjs` (real answers in `tests/fixtures/cloud-*.json`: three lines, a mate, castling in a line, a miss; the key, the 404 cached, errors not, its own 429 back-off), live: off sends nothing, on asks the start position once and shows `cloud depth`, a miss falls back to Stockfish, Prefer cloud leaves Stockfish idle on a cached position, Go deeper on the sample's analysed move asks the cloud only when pressed, and its answer (faked) replaces the Best line and the eval bar's eval.

## Lichess opening explorer

Built 2026-10-28 (`claude/lichess-explorer`, plan `docs/plans/2026-10-28-lichess-explorer.md`). Free board only (the replay has no book panel).

- Settings › **Online** › **Lichess opening explorer** (`TWEAK_DEFAULTS.explorer = false`), with **Database** (Lichess, Masters), **Rating** (Lichess default, all, 1600+, 2000+, 2200+) and **Time control** (Lichess default, all, rapid and slower, blitz and slower) for Lichess games; the default is the website's own, every speed but ultrabullet and every rating group but 400, so the counts match lichess.org's (unfiltered API counts run about 6% higher). Off: the local book, nothing sent.
- On, in the Commentary box where the tablebase doesn't answer and not in a game: the position's name and ECO, the games counted, each move with its count and a white/draw/black bar (click plays it as a drag does), and the top games (links to lichess.org). Offline or backed off: the local book takes its place.
- **Sign-in is required**: the explorer answers 401 without a token (checked 2026-10-28). OAuth2 code + PKCE (`lichess.org/oauth`, `/api/token`, client id `chess-replay`, no scopes, redirect the page's folder) or a pasted personal token; stored in `chess-video.lichess-auth.v1`, sent only to Lichess hosts, kept by Clear saved data like the HF token, removed by Sign out. Signed out, the panel says so and asks nothing of Lichess.
- `LichessCore.explorer` on the shared client (own gate, cache by position and filters, 401 answered as `{ auth: true }` and not cached, 429 back-off). `explorer.lichess.ovh` is in the CSP's `connect-src`, not in `KEEP_HOSTS`.
- **Open**: the response shape follows Lichess's documentation, not a live answer (no token at hand): **verify** on a signed-in run; **verify** that Lichess's OAuth accepts the unregistered client id and the page's redirect URL on the live site.
