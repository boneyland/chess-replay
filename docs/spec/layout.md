# Responsive layout

[Spec](../spec.md) › what is built. How the page fits phones, tablets and desktops (`LayoutCore`).

## Responsive layout

Built 2026-10-26 (designed the same day), replacing the fixed video frames (1920×1080, compact, square, sideways and 840-wide portrait, scaled as a whole to the window) with a normal responsive page. Text keeps one comfortable size whatever the window, extra room goes to the board and the panels together, and nothing is cut off at any size (the anti-goal; a live test sweeps ten sizes for overflow).

- **Where it lives**: `LayoutCore` (`app/layout-core.js`, pure numbers in, boxes `{ x, y, w, h, scroll }` out) says where the board, eval bar, player bars and each panel go for the window's size, the view (`replay`, `free`, `game`, `edit`) and the text size. `Stage` (`app/engine.jsx`) measures the window, picks the column count and gives the views a frame `{ w, h, text, cols, video }` (`useFrame()`). Views place only what `LayoutCore` says; the board is a fixed 768 px picture scaled into its box (`BoardSpace`, `app/game-video-core.jsx`), so pieces, arrows and badges stay in board pixels through `view()`. The fixed `LAYOUTS`/`tallLayout`/`COMPACT`/`SQUARE`/`SHORT` frames are gone.
- **Two columns**: the board (with its player bars, and an eval bar in the replay and free board only) on the left, up to 900 px; on the right the game info (not during a game), then Moves, Commentary under it, then Analysis. Moves wraps the moves in lines, as the Analysis panel's lines do (a light chip for White's move, a dark one for Black's) and is never taller than five lines (30% of the sidebar's room under the game info, 40% in a square-ish window, at least its title and one line), scrolling by whole lines, its height rounded down to whole lines so none shows cut off; Commentary and Analysis share the rest. The eval graph (replay only) runs full width at the bottom (110 px in a viewport from 1181 px wide; 80 px in a narrower one) from 700 px high, and from 670 px wherever the board, held by the width, keeps its size with it there (as short as the room under the board, 80 px at least); otherwise it sits in the sidebar under Analysis (80 px) while the sidebar has the room, else it is hidden; under 670 px high it is always hidden. A square-ish two-column window (at least 0.77 times as tall as wide, as a 760 px wide window from about 640 px high) puts Analysis the full width under the board (at least 160·text px; the board gives way for it), and the eval graph at the bottom under it, full width, 80 px high (as in the sidebar), where Analysis keeps 240·text px over it (at 758 px wide, from about 836 px high), and the sidebar keeps the game info, Moves (40% of the room under the game info, still five lines at most) and Commentary. The sidebar takes at least 320·text px, and up to (230+150)·text+10 px while the board stays at 360 px or more, the width that once held Moves and Commentary side by side.
- **One column**: the board across the width, then the game info (the paused page only: a video has none, nor has a phone held upright, where Moves comes right under the board), Moves (three lines at most, then it scrolls), Analysis and Commentary at the bottom. The paused page flows: each panel is as tall as its contents and the page scrolls (`L.flow`; `LayoutCore`'s one-column heights are only the defaults, which the views don't use; at fixed heights each scrolling inside itself until a1c136e, 2026-10-26) and the transport bar stays pinned at the bottom of the screen. No eval graph (the eval bar stays, where the view has one). Edit position shows only its tray and panel, the tray under the board and the panel under that. On a phone held upright the top player bar sits 2 px from the top of the screen, the player bars are shorter (32·0.8·text px rather than 40·0.8·text) and 1 px from the board (rather than 4), the board, eval bar and player bars 10 px from the screen's sides, and the panels under them run wider, 6 px from the sides, with narrower side padding.
- **Which one**: `LayoutCore.columns()` picks one column when the window is tall for its width (height over 1.25 times the width, with hysteresis: to one column at 1.3, back to two under 1.2) or when the board beside the sidebar would be under 360 px (380 to come back), or when the two-column board, held by the width, would leave over 30% of the window's height empty (25% to come back): by the room left, not by device, so 1024×768 keeps the sidebar, 758×929 keeps it square-ish and 768×1024 stacks. A window too short for the one-column layout keeps two columns where a 200 px board still fits beside the sidebar (a phone held sideways, even at Larger text), and always for a video; a paused page in a narrow, short window (a phone with its keyboard up) takes one column.
- **Game info in a game**: none during a game (vs human or vs computer), in every layout; the panels under it move up (2026-11-08).
- **A short two-column window** (the stage under 500 px tall in two columns, as a phone held sideways; `Stage`'s `short`, `LayoutCore.compute({ dock: true })`): the transport bar docks at the foot of the sidebar under the panels (`L.dock`, 34 px; `.pbar-dock`) rather than running under the whole page, the game info goes and the margins are slimmer (4 px), so the board gets the whole height; the dock's buttons fold into the menu there however wide the window.
- **The transport bar is a bottom bar**: the page's full width down to its foot, square and borderless, in the panel colour (in a short window, the same at the foot of the sidebar, running on to the screen's right and bottom edges, its items padded clear of the margin and any cut-out on the right and centred in its whole height). Its buttons are bare icons (Import game a tray with a down arrow, Menu three lines; their names in the folded menu and for screen readers) that run the bar's full height side by side, so a hover's tint is a block touching its neighbours'; Play/Pause alone sits on a disc in the ink colour.
- **Text size**: Settings › View › Appearance › **Text size** (Small, Default, Large, Larger: 90, 100, 115, 130 %; `TWEAK_DEFAULTS.textSize`, persisted). It sets the root size (`--text`, so the dock, Settings, menus and dialogs follow, all in `rem`) and the panels' text through `tx()`; widths that hold text (the sidebar minimum, the one-column switch, the bars) grow with it, so a larger size stacks sooner. The board's pieces don't scale. The browser's own zoom works too.
- **A video is screen-sized**: while playing or exporting, the page fits the window exactly (the one-column panels shrink under the board, to a 48 px floor, the board never under 200 px) and the title and end cards draw over it, so the size never changes mid-video. Panels follow the current part (Moves the current move), so a long comment can be out of frame at that moment. Exports: [Share and export](export.md).
- **Video touches only while playing or exporting**: the title and end cards, the panels' fade-in, the blunder glow, the camera push and the commentary's typewriter. Stepping, clicking a move, exploring and analysing show a plain, still page, Commentary in whole (a long comment scrolls in its box). Pieces and arrows still slide to their squares, as normal app behaviour.
- **Every view**: the replay, the free board and explorer, Play vs human and Stockfish, and Edit position use the same layout. Overlays (dock, Settings, menus, dialogs) float over it as before, with their rules keyed on `.stage-one` in `app/shell.html`; `.stage-short` (a two-column window under 500 px tall) slims the transport bar, which docks in the sidebar there (above).
- **Touch and drag**: the board's touch layer opts out of the page-wide drag-scroll (`data-no-drag-scroll`), so a mouse drag on the board moves a piece in the one-column page instead of scrolling it.
- **Tests**: `tests/layout-core.test.cjs` (boxes, the column rule, export scale) and the live layout sweep (`tests/live/site.test.cjs`: ten sizes from 320×568 to 2560×1080, 390×400 among them, each at Default and Larger text, in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit, no text overflowing its box, the board's and panels' floors).
